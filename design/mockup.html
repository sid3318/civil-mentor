Fundamental Rights
Articles 12 to 35 of the Constitution
6 min read8 questionsasked in 2025updated 1 Nov 2026

1. Quick anchor

Part III of the Constitution guarantees six groups of rights that a person can enforce against the State. They are justiciable: a person whose right is violated can go straight to the Supreme Court under Article 32, or to a High Court under Article 226.

The trapArticle 21 feels like a citizen's right, but it protects every person on Indian soil, citizen or not.

2. The six groups

GroupArticlesWhat it covers
Right to equality14 to 18Equality before law, equal opportunity in public employment
Right to freedom19 to 22Six freedoms, life and personal liberty, education
Against exploitation23 and 24Forced labour, child labour in hazardous work
